stem.MAP

Stem Cell Protein Complex Map

About

Proteins interact with each other and organize themselves into macromolecular machines (ie. complexes) to carry out functions of the cell. Different cell-types and cellular conditions will affect the ultimate composition of protein complexes but we current lack the identities of complexes present in different conditions. The stem.MAP attempts to address this lack of understanding by integrating co-fractionation mass spectrometry data to obtain a comprehensive view of protein complexes in mouse embryonic stem cells. In stem.MAP we integrated two size exclusion chromatography CF-MS datasets from Mallem et al. and one ion exchange chromatography CF-MS dataset from Cox et al. using a novel prior-based machine learning workflow to produce a stem cell specific complex map with ~4,000 complexes.
